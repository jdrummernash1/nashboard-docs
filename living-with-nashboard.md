# Living with Nashboard

A plain-English guide for everyone in the family, whether you've never used a "smart home" or already live in one.
You don't need to know anything about Home Assistant. These are the small, everyday habits that make the house
quietly helpful.

---

## The one thing to know

**The Home screen shows what matters right now, then gets out of the way.** You don't have to go looking for things.
If the dog needs letting out, rain is coming, a package is out for delivery or a medicine is OK to give again, a card
appears on Home by itself. When it no longer matters, it disappears. If Home looks calm, nothing needs you.

---

## Day one (5 minutes)

1. **Sign in once** on your phone with the Home Assistant app (whoever set up the house can give you a login). The
   dashboard opens inside the app.
2. **Take the welcome tour.** It appears the first time you sign in: choose the pages you want in your menu (hide the
   ones you'll never use), set your alerts, and allow location.
3. **Allow notifications** when your phone asks. That's how the house tells you things when the screen is off (the
   dog's timeout ended, a medicine is OK again, a storm warning).
4. **Allow location.** Weather, radar, traffic and "on the way home" work from where you are. You can turn it off any
   time; a zip code works instead.

That's it. Everything below is optional, a little at a time.

## New everyday habits

These replace things you already do: sticky notes, group texts, "did anyone give her Tylenol?", "is the garage
closed?".

### Glance, don't search
- **Look at Home** when you walk by a screen or pick up your phone. The cards are your to-do list for the house.
- **Tap a card's button** to deal with it right there: Done, Used up, Play, All off, Add gift to list.
- **Tap the fact chip** at the top of the banner for a fun fact about the house. Tap again for another.

### Say it instead of typing
- **Every list has a microphone.** Tap it and say "milk, eggs, bread" to add three things at once.
- The shopping list, chores and pantry all work this way.

### Log it when it happens
- **Medicine:** after you give a dose, tap that medicine's button on the Medicine & fever card (Family Board). It
  records the time and tells everyone when the next dose is OK. Tylenol and ibuprofen take turns; the card shows which
  is up next. You'll get a phone notification when more is OK.
- **Temperature:** type the reading and tap Log temp. Fevers turn red, and a Fever card appears on Home.
- **Symptoms:** one tap for cough, runny nose, rash, threw up, diaper, fluids or nap.
- **For the doctor:** History → **Copy a summary for the doctor** and paste it into a text or the patient portal.
- **Chores:** tick them off on the Family Board or the Home card.
- **Pantry:** add food when you put groceries away (scan the barcode with your phone, or type it) and its expiration
  date. Tap **Used up** when it's gone; the cart button also puts it on the shopping list.

### Let the house remind you
- **Trash night, birthdays, maintenance, the dog, rain, freezing nights, leaving for work:** you don't set reminders,
  the cards just appear.
- **Birthdays:** put "[Name]'s birthday" on the family calendar. Two weeks before, a card offers to add a gift to the
  shopping list.
- **Recipes:** when you send a recipe to the shopping list, it asks what's already in the pantry so you don't buy it
  twice.

### Talk to the whole house
- **Intercom:** on the Family Board, tap Record, say your message (up to 15 seconds) and it plays on every screen in
  the house. It stays quiet while the baby is asleep.

### Something wrong, or an idea?
- **Tap the bug button** (top right) on any page and type or say what's wrong or what you'd like. It goes straight to
  the person who maintains the dashboard. You can attach a photo or screenshot.

## What's new lately

- **Pages open on what matters:** each page's Right now shows only what is live. Everything quiet folds into one line you can open.
- **Today at a glance:** the first card on Home sums up the day in a couple of sentences.
- **Urgent first:** a leak, a power cut or someone at the door is always at the top of Home.
- **Kids page for kids:** grown-up controls sit behind the Grown-ups button and the PIN.
- **More to set up:** pages for things you may not have (a pool, a commute, pets) wait in Menu pages until you tap Set up.
- **Leftovers:** log them on Pantry & Fridge and Home tells you what to eat first.
- **Gigs:** the old Band card plans setup and breakdown times around the show.
- **Scan it:** point the camera at a barcode when adding to shopping, pantry or storage.
- **Wall calendar:** big buttons (and a microphone) to add an event or a list item.
- **Bigger or smaller:** the Interface scale control re-packs everything to fit.

## Good to know

- **Nothing physical happens by accident.** Garage doors and locks always ask you to confirm first, even from the car.
- **The house stays quiet at night.** While the nursery sound machine is playing, the screens don't chime.
- **Every screen updates itself.** You never need to refresh or install anything.
- **Your choices follow you.** The pages you pick and your alert settings go with your login to any device.
- **Guests get their own page** with the Wi-Fi (scan the QR code), contacts and how-tos.
- **On the TV:** tap the cast button to put a rotating dashboard (clock, weather, radar, cameras) on any TV.
- **Holidays:** the banner decorates itself. If the animations bother you, Interface scale → Holiday decorations → Calm or
  Off.

## For grandparents and occasional visitors

If you have a login but only visit sometimes:

- **Use the Home screen and the Family Board.** Everything you need is there: who's home, today's events, chores, the
  shopping list and emergency contacts.
- **Hide everything else** (Menu → Customize menu) so the menu only shows what you use.
- **Make text bigger** with the Interface scale button (on the System page on phones).
- **Babysitting?** The Medicine & fever card shows exactly what was given and when, and when the next dose is OK. Log
  anything you give so the parents see it on their phones.
- **Can't find something?** Tap the bug button and say what you were looking for.
