# Getting started

Your first hour with Nashboard, in the order that works. Installing the two files is on [Set it up](setup.md); this
page starts once the dashboard opens.

<div class="nb-chips"><span class="nb-chip">For the person setting it up</span><span class="nb-chip">Family members can skip to step 6</span></div>

## 1. Take the welcome tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour explains the menu, alerts, location and the cards that come and go. You can
run it again from the **About** page.

## 2. Let your rooms appear

There is nothing to add in Nashboard. Every **area** in Home Assistant becomes a room page automatically, with its
lights, climate, speakers and sensors. If the Rooms page looks empty, put your devices into areas in Home Assistant
(Settings → Areas). On each room page, **Room settings** picks a layout (kitchen, bedroom, garage and so on), hides
sections you do not want and adds cards to that room.

## 3. Work through Getting started

Open **System** (the gear in the top bar) and find the **Setup checklist**. The **Getting started** part lists the
basics: rooms, weather, a calendar, a shopping list, cameras, speakers, phones and a commute. Each row checks Home
Assistant live, says in one line what to add, and opens the page it belongs to. Pages such as Cameras and Calendar show
up in the menu by themselves the moment Home Assistant has a camera or a calendar.

**Optional extras** underneath are just that. Open the list when you are curious; nothing there is required.

## 4. Set up the pages that wait for you

Commute, Pets, Pool, Utilities and Energy stay out of the menu until they have something to show. Open **Settings →
Menu pages**, scroll to **More to set up** and tap **Set up** on the ones your house has. The page opens with its own
set-up steps and joins your menu. Cards that are waiting to be set up on any page are folded into one **More to set up** card at the end of that page, so every page opens on its main content.

## 5. Make Home yours

Home shows a banner of your house, then cards that appear only while they matter. You do not pick them; they come and
go on their own. What you can do:

- **Hide a card** from its ⋯ menu: for today, for good (just for you), or, as an admin, for everyone.
- **Move a card** with Move in the same menu.
- **Collapse a card** by tapping its top bar. Your choice is remembered on every screen you sign in to.
- **Draw your house**: the **Your house** card on the Settings page sets the roof, walls, door, windows and trees of
  the banner house, and can suggest them from a photo.

Every card also lives on its own page (Rain coming on Weather, Trash night on the Family Board), so hiding a Home copy
never loses anything.

## 6. Set up each person

- **Sign in as yourself.** Each Home Assistant user gets their own menu, alerts, accent colour and collapsed cards,
  and they follow that person to every device.
- **Invite the family**: System → Invite someone (admins) creates a Home Assistant user and gives you a welcome
  message to send. Nothing is emailed by the app.
- **Phones**: install the Home Assistant app and sign in. It brings who is home, notifications with the screen off, and
  phone sensors. On the Family Board, each person chooses whether their place shows.
- **Menu pages** (Settings): choose the pages you want and their order.

## 7. Put it on the wall and the TV

- **A wall tablet**: open the dashboard in a kiosk browser and tap **Full window**. Kitchen tablet mode returns to Home
  when idle and dims at night. With a presence or motion sensor, **Wake on approach** (Cast sheet → This device)
  brightens the screen when someone walks up.
- **A TV**: the cast button in the top bar shows a rotating Display view; each TV can have its own setup. A display can
  also be a picture frame when the room is empty.
- **A kid's tablet**: set the start page to Kids and turn on the kid lock (Cast sheet → This device) with a 4-digit
  grown-up PIN.

The full list per device is on the [Device setup checklist](device-setup.md).

## 8. Decide about AI

Nothing needs AI. If Home Assistant has an AI set up, Nashboard uses it for a daily summary, reading photos of school
papers, camera checks and a bedtime story. **Only a local model is used** until an admin switches on Allow cloud AI
under System → AI privacy.

## 9. When something looks wrong

- **Refresh** (top bar) re-reads the page; a long press reloads the app. Your typing is never thrown away.
- The **System** page shows updates, low batteries and anything unavailable.
- **Saved data** (System) lists every saved setting with **Undo** for the last 20 versions.
- The [FAQ](faq.md) answers the common questions, and every card's ⋯ menu has **How this works**.
